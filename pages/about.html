University CSE Capstone Project

Empowering the Next Generation of Software Engineers

The Internship Management Portal connects university Computer Science & Engineering students with premier technology corporations, software houses, and research labs.

Our Mission

To eliminate the placement friction between academic institutions and high-growth technology firms. We ensure every qualifying CSE student gains access to structured, hands-on internships that translate into full-time engineering careers.

Our Vision

To establish a transparent, meritocratic university recruiting standard where students are evaluated on practical software engineering capabilities, problem-solving skills, and academic dedication.

Core Architectural Pillars

Built specifically for high-efficiency university placement drives.

Instant Screening & Matching

Companies can filter thousands of students instantaneously by CGPA thresholds, programming languages, and verified project links.

Transparent Status Timelines

Students trace every stage from initial screening to technical review, interview booking, and official offer dispatch.

Verified Corporate Partners

Every company profile and stipend structure is reviewed to ensure competitive compensation and supportive mentorship.
