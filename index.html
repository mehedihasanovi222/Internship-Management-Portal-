How The Portal Works

A streamlined placement pipeline designed for students and hiring managers.

For CSE Students

1

Create & Complete Profile

Showcase academic CGPA, GitHub repositories, programming skills, and upload your verified PDF resume.

2

Find & Filter Openings

Search through 20+ active roles with work-mode, stipend, duration, and skill filters.

3

1-Click Fast Apply

Attach customized cover letters and submit applications with full timeline status tracking.

4

Interview & Get Hired

Join scheduled video interviews and receive official internship offer letters directly.

For Hiring Companies

1

Create Company Profile

Highlight your company culture, technology stack, office locations, and HR contact details.

2

Post Internships

Publish detailed listings with required skill tags, stipend structures, and deadlines.

3

Screen & Shortlist Applicants

Filter candidates by CGPA, semester, technical skills, and review student resumes instantly.

4

Schedule Interviews & Hire

Book Google Meet/Zoom slots and send instant offer acceptances to top CSE talent.
